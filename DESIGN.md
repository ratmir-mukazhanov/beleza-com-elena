## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene convergence of Korean beauty culture (K-beauty minimalism, ritualistic wellness, high-efficacy botanicals) and the sunlit, tactile elegance of modern Portuguese personal ateliers. Tailored for a discerning European clientele seeking mindful wellness rituals, the interface avoids aggressive commercial retail cues in favor of quiet luxury, breathability, and editorial reverence.

### Visual Style & Direction
- **Warm Minimalist Sanctuary:** Heavy structural whitespace, unhurried pacing, and a serene, warm-ivory canvas reminiscent of hanji paper and Portuguese limestone.
- **Apple-Level Clarity:** Precision typography, disciplined hierarchy, ultra-tactile micro-interactions, and frictionless shopping journeys.
- **Calm Ritualism:** Product showcases prioritize ingredient purity, regimen curation, and personal boutique warmth over hard sales tactics.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in organic balance and warm tactile light. It shuns harsh synthetic starkness in favor of nature-steeped tones.

### Semantic Mapping
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F6F1`):** Warm Ivory. Applied across page viewports and primary backdrops to cultivate an enveloping, non-glare reading environment.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. Reserved for elevated card containers, modal dialogue panels, and active product canvases.
- **Text & Core Contrast (`#242424`):** Charcoal Noir. Provides deep, humanistic contrast for body copy and high-priority titles without the clinical bite of `#000000`.
- **Secondary Tone (`#6F6B65`):** Muted Taupe. Applied to secondary narrative copy, meta labels, ingredient percentages, and supporting subtitles.
- **Primary Herbal Accent (`#87998A`):** Soft Sage. Communicates botanic life, quiet affirmation, and selected interactive states (primary badges, subtle highlight strokes, key links).
- **Tertiary Tone (`#C9A7A1`):** Muted Dusty Rose. Used sparingly for special ritual recommendations, beauty regimen callouts, and limited seasonal curations.
- **Subtle Structural Border (`#D8CDBD`):** Sand Taupe. Serves as hairline dividers, container strokes, and low-contrast borders.

## Typography

The pairing reconciles editorial romance with Swiss-like functional legibility.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Expresses the curated, poetic atmosphere of high-end apothecary traditions. Used for hero phrases, collection titles, and regimen steps. Set in regular or medium weights to preserve delicate serif thins.
- **Structural Text & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine legibility for product descriptions, nutritional/skincare specifications, checkout forms, and tactile buttons.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` - `0.08em`) for Atomy certification tags, volume markers, and step sequences (e.g., `STEP 01: HYDRATE`).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial logic draws inspiration from traditional Korean *hanok* architecture and minimalist gallery curation, prioritizing purposeful emptiness (*yeobaek*) over density.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1320px, `margin` of `3rem`, and `gutter` of `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin` of `2rem` and `gutter` of `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`.

### Vertical Rhythms & Safe Zones
- Section breaks leverage generous vertical padding (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) to evoke an unhurried, spa-like flow.
- Tap targets adhere to a minimum size of 48px × 48px on all handheld devices, surrounded by `space-sm` minimum separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet tonal layering and diffused ambient shadows rather than heavy artificial drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8F6F1` flat ground; absorbs light softly without reflection.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` layered against the ivory ground, delineated by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(216, 205, 189, 0.45)`) and an ultra-subtle warm shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(111, 107, 101, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(111, 107, 101, 0.02)`
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Panels):** Sits slightly higher when focused or hovered:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -6px rgba(111, 107, 101, 0.07), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(111, 107, 101, 0.03)`
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Sheet Drawers):** High-level overlays paired with a warm frosted scrim:
  - Scrim: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.25)`
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The form language mirrors river-smoothed stones and tactile porcelain skincare jars.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Standard Cards, Modals & Sheets:** 16px (`1rem`) to 20px (`1.25rem`), providing a graceful `rounded-2xl` presence.
- **Capsule Elements (Action Buttons, Routine Chips, Pill Navigation):** 9999px fully pill-shaped rounding for effortless organic interaction.
- **Media Displays:** Skincare photography and ingredient imagery employ the standard 16px radius, preserving clean geometric cohesion with parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped, background `#242424`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle hover shift to `#87998A`. Padding: 14px 28px. Font: `label-lg`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill-shaped, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#242424`, hairline border `1px solid #D8CDBD`. Hover shifts background to `#F8F6F1`.
- **Soft Accent CTA:** Pill-shaped, background `#87998A`, text `#FFFFFF`, with hover dimming to opacity 0.9.

### Product & Routine Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` base on `#F8F6F1` background with 16px to 20px corner radii.
- Generous internal padding (24px desktop, 18px mobile).
- Hairline stroke in `rgba(216, 205, 189, 0.45)`.
- Product images rest on neutral, non-distracting off-white cutouts with subtle padding.

### Chips & Tags
- Height: 32px.
- Unselected: Background `#F8F6F1`, border `1px solid #D8CDBD`, text `#6F6B65`.
- Selected / Active: Background `#87998A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #87998A`.
- Regimen / Ritual Tag: Background `rgba(201, 167, 161, 0.18)`, text `#6F6B65`, border none.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Height: 52px to ensure generous mobile touch ease.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #D8CDBD`, text: `#242424`, placeholder: `#6F6B65` with 60% opacity.
- Focus State: Border color `#87998A` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(135, 153, 138, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px × 20px. Rounded (4px for checkboxes, 50% circular for radios).
- Unchecked: White background with `1.5px solid #D8CDBD`.
- Checked: `#87998A` fill with white checkmark icon or inner dot.

### Specialized Boutique Elements
- **Ritual Step Counter:** Circular badge (28px × 28px), background `rgba(135, 153, 138, 0.15)`, text `#87998A`, weight 600.
- **Consultation Banner:** Soft ivory-taupe gradient surface (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8F6F1`), featuring an editorial headline, personalized note from Elena, and an organic floating avatar.
